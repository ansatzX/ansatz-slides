# Editable PPTD poster / infographic

Read only for explicit poster/infographic tasks in this skill, not ordinary decks or unrelated image requests.

## Methodology

Identify the message, audience, viewing/print size, orientation, required information and available assets. Choose a reading order before placing objects. One dominant visual or relationship may organize the page; parallel sections are valid when the content is parallel.

If the direction is unspecified, compare plausible compositions briefly and choose one. Do not require a fixed number of mockups, images or a separate style document for every task. For a complex project, retain the selected rationale in permitted working notes.

## Visual defaults

Typography, palette, image treatment and density should serve the subject and intended viewing distance. Use a stable text safe area over complex imagery. Intentional overlap/cropping of expressive elements must not obscure factual information or make required text unreadable.

Photographs and documentary images carry real-object evidence; externally created diagrams carry relationships. Generated art may illustrate a concept but cannot stand in for an actual observation. Complex illustrations/charts/tables are external assets; PPTD handles framing and native annotations. Do not flatten the entire poster when editable delivery is requested.

## Delivery

Deliver the requested format. If the user wants image output only, keep the editable PPTD project and export/render the image; do not require a PPTX unless requested. If PPTX is requested, follow the standard final-PPTX checks. Do not assume a specific browser, image tool or LaTeX loader.

Check the final output at its actual use size for reading order, clear subject, readable labels, correct sources, crop quality and no unintended overlap. Fix the composition rather than adding decorative filler. Shared evidence and runtime constraints still apply.
