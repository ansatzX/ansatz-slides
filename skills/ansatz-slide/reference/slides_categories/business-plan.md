# Business proposal

## Methodology

Build a clear path from the audience's problem to the proposed solution, supporting evidence, delivery feasibility and explicit ask. Include costs, risks, assumptions and alternatives when needed to judge the proposal.

Claims about customers, adoption, revenue or outcomes need supplied or sourced evidence. Label illustrative scenarios; do not turn aspirations into results. End with the requested decision, resources or next action rather than a decorative conclusion.

## Visual defaults

Use a readable hierarchy between proposition, evidence and detail. Alternate page types when the story benefits; consecutive comparable pages may keep the same layout. Brand identity should come from supplied/verified materials.

Use external diagrams for complex business models or timelines. Native elements frame and annotate; there is no SmartArt authoring capability to assume. Keep typography and motifs consistent without adding decoration to fill space.

## Acceptance

Is the value understandable, the proof traceable, the implementation plausible within stated assumptions, and the ask explicit? Are evidence and aspiration distinguishable?
