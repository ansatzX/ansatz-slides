# Management reporting

## Methodology

Open with the period's overall status. Organize relevant pages as status → evidence → deviation/cause → corrective action → decision needed.

Show target, actual and variance when the source provides them; an unavailable target is a gap, not a number to invent. Keep periods and counting rules consistent. Attribute gaps quantitatively only when the evidence supports the decomposition. Label proposed causes as hypotheses.

Actions should include owner/deadline when known; mark missing ownership rather than inventing commitments. Preserve negative results and unresolved risks.

## Visual defaults

Use stable title and source positions, aligned metrics, and consistent status colors with text labels. External trend charts, progress graphics and tables carry the data; captions explain implications. Do not remove axes or labels necessary to understand them for visual uniformity.

Functional cards or parallel columns may group projects. Density follows the reporting audience; no decorative quota or forced page-type rotation applies.

## Acceptance

Can the reader tell how the period went, what supports that status, which issues remain, who needs to act and what decisions are requested?
